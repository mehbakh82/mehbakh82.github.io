Computer vision

Satellite object detection

I fine-tuned and evaluated a 23-class YOLOv10s detector on a merged aerial-imagery corpus of 85,977 files. I used inverse-frequency class weighting and augmentation that accounted for object rotation and scale.

Persian license plate recognition

I built a two-stage YOLOv12m pipeline that detects plates and then localizes individual characters. I consolidated and normalized IR-LPR, IMLP, ILPD, and 83,844 Iranis character crops.

Computer vision

Aerial, document, and image systems

I developed an aerial detector ensemble across VisDrone, OpenImages, and DOTA, using SAHI slicing and altitude-adaptive confidence fusion. I also developed layout-aware OCR with Chandra, face analysis with SCRFD and ArcFace, and image tagging with RAM++ and Persian label localization.

Speech and language

Persian speech recognition

I fine-tuned a 116.5-million-parameter NeMo Conformer Hybrid RNNT/CTC on 997,540 utterances covering 1,601 hours. I used source-group-disjoint splits, replay training, augmentation, and Persian text normalization.

0.00124

Speech and language

Offline multilingual services

I packaged NeMo and Seamless M4T speech recognition, Pyannote and Sortformer diarization, VoxLingua language identification, NLLB-200 translation, and VITS speech synthesis in self-contained Docker services for CPU and GPU. The portfolio also covers zero-shot text classification and explainable Persian offensive-content moderation with ParsBERT.

ML infrastructure

GPU model serving and observability

I deployed and stress-tested quantized vLLM and pooling services on H100 GPUs for Qwen 27B, GPT-OSS 20B, Gemma 12B, and Qwen3 embedding and reranking models up to 8B. I built GPU memory planning, monitoring, alerting, and distributed tracing with Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, and Jaeger.
