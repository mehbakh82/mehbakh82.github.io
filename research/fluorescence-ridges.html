Question

Can a faint fluorescent ridge serve as a reference if its peak moves between independent exposures? The study tested point repeatability before comparing denoisers.

What I tested

Across seven eligible FMD development fields, median point agreement was 36.4%. A follow-up on six development fields compared short same-path segments with matched parallel controls and found 74.0% versus 15.9% support.

Decision and follow-up

The point gate stopped the denoiser comparison. No external W2S field had enough segments for the follow-up endpoint, leaving it unestimable. The development finding is exploratory and leaves individual filament identity unresolved.
