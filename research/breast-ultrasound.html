Recoverable errors in breast ultrasound

A fixed-budget segmentation pilot asked which first-pass masks a second pass could improve.

Question

Can we route a second segmentation pass to the cases most likely to benefit? The study held the first-pass segmenter, refiner, and retry budget fixed so that only the allocation policy changed.

What I tested

The held-out development pilot comprised 365 BUS-BRA scans from 214 patients. I compared first-pass masks with 73 entropy-selected retries and the best possible 73 allocations using reference masks as an oracle.

Decision and follow-up

Oracle headroom fell below the prespecified threshold, so router training stopped. The internal test fold and external datasets remained unscored. In a post-hoc audit, 19 entropy-selected retries worsened their masks. All 19 contained the full reference lesion in the crop, and 16 added false-positive pixels.
